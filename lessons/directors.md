Use the organizational meeting to connect each office with actual duties. A director’s experience helps, but the board acts through its legal authority and governing documents.

## Essentials

- Separate the board’s collective authority from each officer’s assigned role. A president is not automatically authorized to make every association decision alone. Document delegated authority and its limits.
- Identify the secretary’s records and notice duties, the treasurer’s financial oversight, and the president’s and vice president’s responsibilities in the bylaws. A management contract should clarify who performs each task. Review directors and officers (D&O) insurance with the broker: who is insured, defense costs, limits, exclusions and claim-notice deadlines. General liability and fidelity/crime coverage serve different purposes; do not assume one substitutes for another.
- Directors should act in good faith, with appropriate care, and in the association’s interests. Disclose potential conflicts and follow the specific statutory process for interested transactions.
- Confirm committee appointments and education records during the organizational cycle. Initial certification has its own deadline; a new director should not wait until next October for required training.

## Key mistake

Treating the president’s preference as a board decision or assuming an officer title gives unlimited authority.

## Board discussion

After the annual meeting, a new president tells the manager that all contracts under $5,000 can be signed without a board vote. Nobody can locate a resolution authorizing that limit.

- Where would you look for spending and signing authority?
- How should a lawful delegation be defined and recorded?

Review the bylaws, statutes, adopted budget, contracts and any valid delegation. Do not infer a new spending power from the office title alone. If appropriate, the board can consider a specific delegation through the proper decision process.

## Self-paced work

Does the new president’s title establish the $5,000 signing limit?

- Yes, automatically
- No; locate the applicable authority or valid delegation
- Yes, when acting as a volunteer

Answer: No; locate the applicable authority or valid delegation

Look to law, governing documents, budgets and valid board decisions rather than assuming individual authority.

If a delegation is appropriate, what should it specify?

- Scope, spending limits, reporting and the proper approving decision
- Unlimited discretion without records
- That all future contracts are automatically approved

Answer: Scope, spending limits, reporting and the proper approving decision

A clear authorized delegation identifies the work, limit and accountability and is recorded through the proper process.

Explain where you would verify authority and how a lawful delegation could define scope, limits and reporting.

## Knowledge check

Does becoming president automatically grant unlimited individual spending authority?

- Yes
- No
- Only for maintenance

Answer: No

Authority comes from the applicable law, documents and valid board decisions.

Should a new director wait for next October’s lesson to meet the initial certification deadline?

- No
- Yes
- Only if unpaid

Answer: No

The statutory initial-certification deadline remains separate from this seasonal pilot.
