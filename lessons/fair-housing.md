An exception request connected with a disability deserves the appropriate fair-housing review. Consistency includes recognizing when the law requires an individualized accommodation.

## Essentials

- A request does not have to use a particular legal phrase before it deserves attention. Identify whether the resident is seeking an accommodation or modification connected with a disability.
- Assistance animals are not treated simply as ordinary pets under the applicable fair-housing framework. Do not apply a pet rule mechanically without reviewing the request.
- Protect sensitive information, request only appropriate documentation, and involve qualified counsel where the standard or facts are uncertain. Avoid public discussion of private medical details.
- Finish the education year by reconciling each director’s approved-course certificates separately from this pilot’s practice records. Pilot quiz completion is not proof of approved CE compliance.

## Key mistake

Automatically rejecting a disability-related request because the resident’s proposal conflicts with an ordinary rule.

## Board discussion

A resident asks for an exception to an animal restriction because of a disability. A director wants the resident to explain the diagnosis during an open board meeting.

- What information is appropriate to handle privately?
- How should the request be evaluated and recorded?

Use an appropriate confidential accommodation process and qualified guidance. A public explanation of medical details is not a sound default. Preserve the decision record without unnecessary disclosure.

## Self-paced work

Where should disability-related information be handled?

- Through an appropriate confidential process
- In an open meeting by default
- On the public course website

Answer: Through an appropriate confidential process

Request only appropriate information and protect sensitive details. Do not require public disclosure of a diagnosis as the default.

How should the animal exception request be evaluated?

- Apply the pet restriction automatically
- Ask residents to vote on the diagnosis
- Use the applicable accommodation framework and qualified guidance

Answer: Use the applicable accommodation framework and qualified guidance

An assistance-animal request requires individualized review under the relevant fair-housing framework.

Describe an appropriate private next step and a useful decision record that avoids unnecessary medical details. Do not enter real resident information.
