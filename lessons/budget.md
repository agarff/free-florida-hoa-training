Move this lesson before the association’s actual budget process. The goal is to understand the relationship between the plan, cash, obligations and financial reports.

## Essentials

- An annual budget is a plan; bank cash is a balance at a point in time. Neither alone explains every unpaid obligation or future replacement need. Read them together with financial statements and commitments.
- Compare actual spending with the approved budget and ask about significant differences. Check unpaid invoices, receivables, reserve balances and contract renewals before assuming a surplus is available.
- The required annual financial-report level depends on statutory criteria, including revenues. Do not assume every small association is exempt or that a report of receipts and expenditures is always sufficient.
- Assign responsibility for the budget, report preparation, owner delivery and review. Use a calendar and obtain appropriate accounting help for unfamiliar items.

## Key mistake

Treating the bank balance as money available to spend without checking reserves, bills and commitments.

## Board discussion

The bank balance is $25,000 and a director proposes a $10,000 landscaping upgrade. The balance includes reserve money and invoices that have not yet cleared.

- What additional figures are needed before deciding affordability?
- What would you ask the manager or accountant to explain?

Identify restricted reserves, outstanding obligations, expected receipts and authorized spending before deciding. A cash balance does not establish that money is unrestricted or that the proposed project has been authorized.

## Self-paced work

### Financial literacy and transparency

Example: $25,000 cash minus $12,000 restricted reserves and $4,000 unpaid bills leaves $9,000 before other commitments. That does not authorize spending. Under §720.303(7), revenue generally determines the report: below $150,000, receipts/expenditures; $150,000–under $300,000, compilation; $300,000–under $500,000, review; $500,000 or more, audit. At least 1,000 parcels requires an audit regardless of revenue. Check lawful higher-reporting votes, permitted reductions and owner-delivery deadlines.

What must be checked before treating $25,000 cash as available?

- Only whether the online bank balance is current
- Only whether the project is popular
- Reserve restrictions, unpaid obligations, expected receipts and spending authority

Answer: Reserve restrictions, unpaid obligations, expected receipts and spending authority

A cash balance alone does not establish unrestricted surplus or authorization to spend it.

What is a useful next step before approving the landscaping upgrade?

- Spend now and reconcile later
- Ask for a cash/obligation reconciliation and budget comparison
- Assume reserves are ordinary operating cash

Answer: Ask for a cash/obligation reconciliation and budget comparison

Reconcile the financial picture and authority before deciding affordability. Obtain accounting help where needed.

## Knowledge check

Does the checking-account balance by itself establish spendable surplus?

- No
- Yes
- Yes, if statements are current

Answer: No

Check obligations, restrictions, reserves and the budget as well.

What helps determine the annual reporting level?

- The president’s preference alone
- The applicable statutory criteria, including revenues
- Whether the meeting lasted an hour

Answer: The applicable statutory criteria, including revenues

Apply the reporting criteria to the actual association.
