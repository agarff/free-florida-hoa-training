A new education year is a useful moment to organize the records that explain what the association decided, spent and approved. Transparency works best as a routine, with privacy built into the process.

## Essentials

- Build an index of governing documents, adopted rules, meeting minutes, contracts, financial records and architectural decisions. Record the current version and the person responsible for maintaining each category.
- Owner access is governed by Chapter 720, including deadlines and exclusions. A document being convenient to post does not mean every part of it is appropriate for a public website. Separate public learning resources from private association files.
- Use a consistent intake process for records requests: record receipt, identify responsive records, check exclusions, document the response and retain evidence of delivery. Do not use scattered personal inboxes as the only archive.
- For board education, track initial certification separately from annual continuing education. A course being useful is different from its being approved for credit.

## Key mistake

Losing track of an owner’s records request—or releasing information that should stay private.

## Board discussion

An owner requests the landscaping contract, the latest financial report and every email concerning a neighbor’s disability accommodation. The manager proposes placing the whole folder on a publicly accessible drive.

- Which records need review before disclosure?
- How would you document the response without exposing the neighbor’s medical information?

Identify and provide the accessible association records through the applicable access process. Review protected and excluded information before sharing; disability-related material does not become public merely because it is in an association folder. Keep a request log and consult the relevant statutory exclusions.

## Self-paced work

### Recordkeeping and owner access

Under §720.303(4)–(5), retain applicable official records generally at least seven years, subject to specific rules and longer document requirements. Provide access within ten business days after a written request. Keep governing documents, minutes and financial records organized; separate excluded medical, privileged and protected identifying information. Do not require an owner to explain the purpose of inspection.

What is a sound first response to a records request?

- Wait until the next annual meeting
- Log receipt, identify the requested records and calendar the applicable response period
- Reject it because staff are busy

Answer: Log receipt, identify the requested records and calendar the applicable response period

A request log and deadline help the association respond through the applicable statutory process.

How should a file containing restricted personal information be handled?

- Separate protected information and provide the accessible records appropriately
- Publish the whole file online
- Withhold every association record permanently

Answer: Separate protected information and provide the accessible records appropriately

Apply the statutory exclusions carefully while preserving the right to inspect accessible official records.

Describe the request log, privacy review and response evidence you would keep for the guide’s hypothetical.

### Florida director education requirements · optional reference

Initial education: §720.3033(1)(a) requires a certificate from a department-approved education provider within 90 days after election or appointment, repeated at least every four years. The named curriculum topics are financial literacy and transparency, recordkeeping, levying fines, and notice and meeting requirements.

Annual continuing education is separate: at least four hours for directors of HOAs with fewer than 2,500 parcels, including this fictional 54-home association; at least eight hours for 2,500 or more parcels. Useful training material is not automatically an approved course.

This pilot proposes both hybrid annual CE and a separate self-paced BMC track for initial and four-year certification. Both pilot paths are available to explore; neither is approved. Annual CE is additional to certification, including certification years; one completion is not automatically credit for both. The monthly guides revisit foundational subjects and additional practical topics, including insurance. D&O is not a separately named mandatory topic in §720.3033(1)(a)4.
