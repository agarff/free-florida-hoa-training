# Security reporting

Send security or privacy reports privately through the maintainer contact configured for the site you use. The original site links to its private contact route from the privacy page. For an independent copy, use the channel published by that copy's maintainer.

Include the affected page, a brief description, perceived impact and a safe way to contact you. Use a local copy and synthetic data when possible. Do not post reports to public suggestions, include another person’s private data, or send credentials or session material. Give the maintainer time to investigate before public disclosure.
