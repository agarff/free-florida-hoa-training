# Code of conduct

Discuss the material and evidence respectfully. Welcome different levels of experience and explain disagreements with sources where possible. Use plain language and offer specific corrections.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, impersonation, spam and disclosure of private information are grounds for withholding or removing contributions. The maintainer may close or hide discussions that breach these standards.

Contact your site’s maintainer through a private channel for moderation concerns. Share only the information needed to describe the concern.
